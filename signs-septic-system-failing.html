The Warning Signs

← Back to Glens Falls Septic home

1. Slow drains throughout the house

One slow sink is usually a clogged pipe. Every drain running slow — tubs, showers, toilets — at the same time points to the septic tank or the line leaving your house. Often it means the tank is full or the outlet is blocked.

2. Sewage odors indoors or in the yard

A rotten-egg or sewage smell near drains, around the tank, or over the drain field usually means gases aren't venting properly or untreated effluent is surfacing. If it smells like sewage, the system is telling you something.

3. Wet, soggy, or extra-lush spots over the drain field

Patches of grass that are suddenly greener, taller, or squishy underfoot over the leach field can mean effluent is pushing up to the surface instead of filtering down through the soil. This is one of the classic early signs of field trouble.

4. Gurgling sounds in pipes

Bubbling or gurgling from toilets and drains when water runs elsewhere in the house means air is trapped in the system — often because the tank is full or a line is restricted. It frequently shows up before a full backup.

5. Backups in toilets, tubs, or floor drains

Sewage coming back up is the sign nobody wants — and the one that causes the most damage. It usually means the tank is overfull, the outlet filter is clogged, or the drain field can't accept more water.

6. Standing water around the tank or field

Puddles that linger over the tank, the distribution box, or the drain field — especially in dry weather — suggest the system is discharging at or near the surface instead of treating and dispersing wastewater below ground.

What to Do First

  • Cut back water use — fewer showers, no laundry loads, shorter flushes. Every gallon you add to a struggling system makes it worse.
  • Don't use chemical drain cleaners — they kill the bacteria your tank needs and can damage pipes and components.
  • Note what you see — which fixtures are affected, where the smell or wetness is, and when it started. This helps us diagnose faster when you call.
